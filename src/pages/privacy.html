Privacy

Privacy & our no-data-selling pledge

You can use BenefitDial without an account, newsletter sign-up or callback request. We don't ask for your name, email address or phone number to use our tools. The calculator code processes your entries on your device. Hosting and analytics services may receive technical visit information, as explained below.

Last updated: November 12, 2026

What our tools don't ask for

BenefitDial does not use account registration or lead-generation forms. Our tools do not ask for:

  • Your name. There's nowhere to enter it.
  • Your street address. The Medicare tool asks you to choose a state and county to filter public plan data in your browser. The tool's code does not submit those selections to a server.
  • Your email. There's no sign-up, no newsletter, and no "enter your email to see results." If you choose to contact us by email, your email address and message are included in that correspondence.
  • Your phone number. Ever. There is no "talk to a licensed agent," no callback request, and no one will call you because you visited this site.
  • Any account or login. There are no passwords because there are no accounts.

The calculators run entirely in your browser

When you type your Social Security benefit into the COLA calculator or choose your plan in the What Changed tool, the math happens on your own device. Our calculator code does not submit those entries to a server, add them to analytics events or save them in browser storage. Your browser may separately retain form values or restore a tab. Saved text-size preferences and checklist progress are described below.

Analytics and technical information

Our pages include a Google Analytics 4 (GA4) script supplied by Google for traffic measurement. A browser that loads this script contacts Google and sends connection information such as its IP address and browser details. Loading the script does not, by itself, establish that a visit was recorded or a cookie was stored.

When GA4 measurement runs and browser storage is available, it can use first-party analytics cookies on benefitdial.com, including _ga to distinguish browsers and _ga_<container-id> to maintain session state. Google is an external service provider, but cookies stored for our domain are first-party cookies. Actual collection and cookie creation depend on the site's configuration and browser restrictions. These identifiers are not the same as anonymous page totals. See Google's explanation of GA4 cookies.

Cloudflare delivers and protects this site and may process technical request information, including IP addresses, requested URLs and browser details. Delivered pages also include a Cloudflare Web Analytics performance beacon. Cloudflare describes this beacon as measuring page performance without cookies or browser storage; whether it runs depends on the page's security policy and browser restrictions. See Cloudflare's beacon privacy information. This is separate from technical information processed to deliver and secure the site.

What we will never do

This is the heart of our pledge. BenefitDial is not an insurance broker or agent. We take no commissions from insurance carriers and do not operate a lead-generation service. We will never:

  • Sell your contact details or calculator entries. We do not ask for contact details to use the tools or package calculator entries for sale. Technical information handled by hosting and analytics providers is described above; this pledge is not a claim that no information reaches any service provider.
  • Generate or sell sales leads. You are a reader, not a lead. There is no list, and nothing about you is packaged and sold.
  • Hand your information to a call center or an insurance broker. No one is standing by. When you're ready to enroll, you go straight to the official Medicare Plan Finder or call 1-800-MEDICARE. We never recommend a specific plan for payment.

Cookies and what's stored in your browser

A cookie is a small value stored by your browser for a website. The GA4 cookies described above are first-party cookies when stored for benefitdial.com, even though Google's script creates them. We do not promise that the site is cookie-free or that every visit creates a cookie. Possible future advertising cookies are separate from this analytics disclosure. You can delete or block cookies in your browser; the calculators do not use cookies to do their math.

Our interface also uses localStorage, which is separate from cookies, for these two features:

  • Text size: bc-textsize stores your selected text size so it can be restored on a later visit.
  • Open Enrollment checklist: benefitdial-oehub-checks stores which checklist items you have checked so your progress can be restored.

Our interface code does not transmit these saved settings to a server. They remain in this browser until you change them, clear the site's data or the browser removes them. They are not an account or a cross-device sync. Clearing cookies alone may leave localStorage intact; use your browser's option to clear this site's stored data to remove both settings. If storage is blocked, the controls can still work during the visit, but your choices may not be saved.

Children

BenefitDial is intended for adults, primarily people approaching or already in retirement. It is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Your choices

You have simple, practical control over your privacy here:

  • Analytics controls. The site currently has no analytics consent banner or on-site analytics toggle. Browser privacy tools can limit script requests or cookies. Blocking cookies alone does not necessarily block requests to service providers.
  • Do Not Track. Our current site code does not use this signal to switch analytics on or off.
  • Advertising choices. There are no live ads to personalize here today. Provider-specific choices will be described before advertising is enabled.
  • Cookies and stored settings. Your browser's controls can clear cookies and the site's stored data, including both the text-size preference and checklist progress.
  • No account to close. Using the tools creates no account or newsletter subscription. You can contact us about privacy without providing calculator values or medical information.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the site works in a way that affects your privacy, we'll update this page and its "Last updated" date. Changes to analytics, browser storage or advertising require a fresh review of these disclosures. Our tools will remain separate from insurance sales and lead generation.
